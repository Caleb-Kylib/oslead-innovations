Our History & Values

About Oslead Innovations

We combine smart technology with standard animal husbandry to supply farmers and cooperatives with premium hatchery products.

Growing Success Since 2012

Oslead Innovations was born from a desire to modernize incubation success parameters. Initially starting as a small breeding house, we identified that the biggest hurdle facing farmers was high early chick mortality and low incubator reliability.

By engineering optimized microcomputer incubator designs and strictly maintaining standard grandfather-level breeder selection criteria, we solved those bottlenecks. Today, we are proud to support thousands of active livestock entrepreneurs across the country.

Our Mission

To supply farmers with healthy day-old chicks, premium incubation tools, and knowledge to double their yield outputs.

Our Vision

To build the most trusted, bio-secure smart hatchery network in East Africa, powered by AgriTech innovations.

Core Values

Strict Bio-security protocols, constant design iteration, transparency, and lifelong customer success orientation.

Our Timeline

How we grew from a small local farm hatchery into an industry-leading agricultural innovations provider.

2012

Oslead Innovations begins operations with a simple hatchery unit producing 1,000 chicks per month.

2016

We transition to manufacturing custom microcomputer automated incubators for local breeders.

2020

Establishment of our regional distribution center, delivering healthy day-old chicks nationwide.

2026

We expand our smart IoT hatchery product lines, achieving 98% average hatch rates across client farms.

Security & Bio-Safety

Quality Assurance Protocols

At Oslead Innovations, we take biosecurity seriously. Our facilities undergo continuous sanitization schedules. Every parent stock egg is scanned for hairline defects, and day-old chicks are examined and vaccinated before dispatch.

  • ✓ Fully sanitized logistics transport chick carrying boxes.
  • ✓ Microprocessor temperature check validation of storage logs.
  • ✓ Routine veterinary pathogen screening checks on all parent breeding stock.
